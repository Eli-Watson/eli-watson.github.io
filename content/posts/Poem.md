“Spare not the rod”

Walking to a city on a hill
Praising the lord as he always will
But as Elisha reached the top
The shouts of children made him stop
As they mocked the lack of hair on top
“Bald Head” “Bald Head” they cried
Malice in their little eyes
A lesson needed to be learned
Due to disrespect unearned
A prayer to the lord cursed the rebels
He sent a beast like jezebel
Two giant momma bears emerged
Forty two young boys they caught
Mauling them without a thought
The other, they ran and fled
Never forgetting his bald head
Unjust ridicule now corrected
The lords prophet always protected
The wrath of the Lord on full display
About His word He does not play
